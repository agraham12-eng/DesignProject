What is a CPU?

A CPU also known as (Central Processing Unit) is the "brain" of a computer. It is the main component that gives out instructions, does calculations, and manages the whole computer. It processes everything like a keystroke and all the complex software applications. The better the CPU the faster you computer does commands and thus the speed of simple tasks like opening a new window. CPU and RAM work together as the RAM is how much your computer can do at once so if you have a really good CPU but bad RAM they wont be able to keep up. Thus you need to get a balance. When checking for CPU speed check the clock speed and cores.

CORES

CPU cores are added for faster speed and more efficiency. They help open applications with efficient speed, and use themselves to speed up application processes, such as video editing or streaming.

Clock speed

Clock speed is used for the efficiency and speed of the CPU. Like other clock speeds such as the GPU and RAM, the CPU clock speed speeds up application processes and make everything feel faster and smoother. Clock speed can also be slowed down and sped up.
