What is a GPU?

A GPU (Graphics Processing Unit) is one of the most important parts of a gaming computer. It controls graphics, FPS, resolution, and overall gaming performance.

Better GPUs allow smoother gameplay, higher graphics settings, faster rendering, and improved video editing performance.

RTX 4090

Top of the line GPU designed for professional editing, high quality gaming, and Video recording and Streaming.

RTX 4070

Mid to High range GPU, made for medium to high graphically demanding games. Used for streaming as well as video editing for content creation.

RX 7800 XT

Price to performance high-end GPU. Used for AMD GPU made for high end gaming, streaming, video editing.
